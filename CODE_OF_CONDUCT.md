# Community standards

Kerneon welcomes careful disagreement and evidence-led criticism. Treat other contributors with respect, assume good faith, and challenge ideas with reproducible information rather than personal attacks.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, deliberate disruption, publication of private information and unsafe instructions presented as fact are not acceptable. Security-sensitive findings belong in a private vulnerability report, not a public demonstration against an unpatched user base.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that breach these standards and may restrict participation when necessary to keep the community safe and useful. Serious concerns can be raised privately through GitHub’s security-reporting channel.
